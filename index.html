🖥 How to display on an external screen

The wheel is designed for projectors, TVs, LED displays, or a second monitor.

  1. Connect your external display.
  2. Drag this browser window onto the external display.
  3. Press the Present button or press F.
  4. Press Space to spin.
  5. Press H to hide or show the interface.
  6. Press Esc to exit Presentation mode.
